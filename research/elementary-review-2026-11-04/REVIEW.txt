Assessment
----------
No mathematical error was found in the final fifteen-source synthesis or the
new closing arguments examined. Taken with the previously established branch
lemmas, they do close the elementary threshold argument. The conclusion does
not require the complete fourteen-source classification or its invariant.
This is a written-proof review, not a Lean verification, and not a fresh
line-by-line audit of every older note in the full dependency chain.

The final split really covers every opposite total, including the boundaries:
- o=3,4: there are at least eleven adjacent sources; retain three opposites
  and eleven adjacent sources. The three-opposite lemma includes all three
  multiplicity patterns, including three identical varieties.
- o=5,6: retain five opposites and nine adjacent sources. Cap three implies
  at least three occupied adjacent classes. If not all nine are distinct,
  select two A and one each of two other classes for the helper lemma.
- o=7: the seven-opposite-plus-adjacent theorem applies.
- o>=8: choose eight opposite sources, preserving the cap of two.
The concentrated opposite cases are excluded before these caps are used.
The two deletion identities in that reduction are correct.

The two-source compression is valid for both target types. The removed
sources are unavailable to every other placement; a virtual opposite source
can only supply a tricoloured corner, and only one removed physical source
is needed to replace it. Disjoint cuts really contain an opposite variety.
The triangle/star proof covers small or empty signed families as well as
maximal supports. No threshold theorem is used in this geometric step.

The one-opposite closure correctly addresses the earlier missing existence
of a second triple: the thirteen-source 3,2,2,2,2,1,1 lemma rules out the only
possible unique-triple profile. Promotion is then applied only to a triple
with its required outsider matching. The avoiding-opposite alternatives cover
all five possible varieties in three symmetry types.

The three-opposite branches use their local matching and common-corner
lemmas, not the later two-opposite theorem. The latter uses the former by
compression, so this joining step is not circular. The common-corner and
nine-source repeated-colour completions preserve physical-copy counts.

Editorial consequence
---------------------
The archive supports a credible complete elementary route, but it is not yet
a short or consolidated proof. The selected 22 synthesis/new-closing notes
alone contain 24,769 whitespace-separated words, including repetition and
excluding the older supporting chain. This is not a proposed final word count.

Prepare one self-contained dependency-ordered document, extracting only the
lemmas used by the direct fifteen-source proof. Remove historical status and
audit prose from its mathematical narrative, state the general restoration
and small Hall-cover principles once, and put fixed tables in an appendix.
Evaluate the resulting length before replacing the current manuscript proof.
Do not describe the elementary route as Lean-verified.

The classification replay continues separately. An elementary proof of N5=15
would not establish the 11,430 classification or invariant. No production
paper, production Lean proof, or running certificate source was changed by
this review.

Assessment
----------
Most remaining length belongs to distinct concentration, private-corner and
mixed-source arguments. Replacing that structure with a much shorter proof
is more than copy-editing. I would not continue an open-ended effort to fit
it into this article. The current paper already offers the sharp cuboid
classification, geometric extensions, explicit obstructions, an algorithm
and separate formal verification.

Preserve the elementary draft and its useful constructions. A compact lemma
replacing an entire family of cases could justify reopening the publication
decision; another small percentage reduction alone would not. A separate
elementary-proof paper is an option to assess after further consolidation,
not a recommendation to publish the present 154 pages as they stand.

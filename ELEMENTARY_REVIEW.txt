Elementary proof of the five-colour corner threshold: external review package
============================================================================

Branch: research/elementary-proof-review
Base: 1dce44d24f76e84f0266025ab9ce0ab94a306873
Prepared: 4 November 2026

Purpose
-------
This branch preserves the proposed elementary proof of N5=15 and our efforts
to consolidate and shorten it. We would welcome a second opinion on its
correctness, its dependency structure, and especially whether a better
organising principle could replace whole families of cases.

The publication paper and Lean sources at the repository root are unchanged.
The elementary draft is separate under research/. It is NOT the proof
formalised by the existing Lean development, and this branch is NOT a claim
that the entire elementary argument has received an independent audit.

Current assessment
------------------
The supplied research notes present a complete direct fifteen-source route.
Review of the synthesis and selected component arguments found no error,
but the full older dependency chain has not been checked line by line.
The extraction reduced to 66 retained arguments, approximately 72,321 words,
and a 154-page mathematical appendix, accompanying a 24-page draft article.
That is too long for the intended replacement in the current paper.

Our recommendation so far is to keep the compact certified corner proof as
the publication route. This review branch preserves the elementary work so
that another reader can assess both the argument and the possibility of a
substantial structural simplification. The assessments are working judgments,
not a finding that a short elementary proof is impossible.

Suggested reading order
-----------------------
1. research/elementary-paper/ASSESSMENT.txt
   Measured outcome of the bounded editorial pass and its limitations.

2. research/elementary-paper/paper/five-colour-cubes.pdf
   Proposed 24-page article. Its elementary corner section gives the final
   synthesis and locates the supporting appendix arguments.

3. research/elementary-paper/paper/five-colour-elementary-appendix.pdf
   The current 154-page mathematical appendix. For source-level review use
   elementary-appendix.md or the TeX alongside it. Necessary proofs belong
   here; the appendix is not intended as optional computational material.

4. research/elementary-paper/DEPENDENCIES.txt and appendix-manifest.json
   Recorded dependencies, original-note hashes, editorial override hashes,
   and retained sections. The recorded graph is ordered and acyclic; that
   does not certify every implicit mathematical dependency.

5. research/elementary-review-2026-11-04/REVIEW.txt
   What was actually read and checked during the earlier proof review.
   The extracted supplied notes and their exploratory programs are in its
   research/ subdirectory. These contain historical claims and unfinished
   routes; do not read every historical 'open' or 'complete' as current status.

6. research/elementary-organising/RESULTS.txt and FOLLOWUP.txt
   Subsequent bounded attempts at a better organising principle. The sharp
   thirteen-source two-completion lemma has exhaustive computational checks
   and reductions, but still no short elementary proof of its nested case.
   three-witness-lemma.txt is a short written elementary reduction obtained
   in the latest attempt. These results are NOT incorporated into the draft.

The separate six-page certificate-supplement.pdf in the draft paper directory
preserves the alternative certificate proof. The root paper/ remains the
publication baseline and can be consulted for comparison.

Questions for a reviewer
------------------------
* Does the direct fifteen-source synthesis use the exact hypotheses proved
  by its components? Are there hidden circular dependencies or omitted cases?
* Do the geometric tables have adequate completeness arguments, and do all
  placements preserve distinct physical sources and proper rotations?
* Can compression, repeated-colour restoration, private-corner geometry,
  or matching structure replace a substantial branch of the proof?
* Is the fixed six-source nested configuration a productive simplification
  target? Could its thirteen-source lemma have a short geometric proof?
* Given the complete dependency burden, would the elementary route improve
  this paper, or should selected structural lemmas be retained separately?

A shorter computer-assisted proof would also be a useful outcome. We prefer
explanatory lemmas and a small auditable calculation to another long hand
classification. New numerical searches alone are not the main review goal.

Scope and provenance
--------------------
* This is a review snapshot, not a submission version or verification release.
* The raw notes are preserved; editorial changes are separate in
  research/elementary-paper/overrides/. Regeneration uses those overrides.
* The original research ZIP's SHA-256 is recorded in ARCHIVE.sha256; its
  extracted contents are included, without caches or build logs. The ZIP
  itself is unnecessary for the provided regeneration commands.
* The fourteen-cube classification, its invariant, and its separate Lean
  replay are outside this package. Their historical mentions in the draft
  are not claims about their present verification status.
* No new elementary-proof Lean formalisation is included.
* AI-assisted development/review notes are not independent human refereeing.
* Some exploratory programs in the raw archive assume old absolute paths
  or optional packages. The documented commands below are the supported
  local reproduction route; this is not a promise to replay every experiment.

Reproduction from a fresh checkout of this branch
------------------------------------------------
Reading the PDFs needs no software setup. To regenerate and build them, use
Python 3, Pandoc, latexmk, and a TeX installation with the packages used by
the checked-in TeX (the same article packages, plus Pandoc's standard output).

  python3 research/elementary-paper/tools/assemble_article.py
  python3 research/elementary-paper/tools/assemble_appendix.py
  latexmk -cd -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error \
    research/elementary-paper/paper/five-colour-cubes.tex \
    research/elementary-paper/paper/five-colour-elementary-appendix.tex \
    research/elementary-paper/paper/certificate-supplement.tex

The generators read this checkout, not another repository or a local ZIP.
The current overrides are already included; rerunning the historical editing
script simplify_dependencies.py is not required for reproduction.

Selected supplementary checks (Python standard library only):

  python3 research/elementary-organising/check_two_loops_independent.py
  python3 research/elementary-organising/check_three_witnesses.py
  python3 research/elementary-paper/tools/check_restored_extension.py
  python3 research/elementary-paper/tools/check_butterfly_completions.py

These check specific finite statements; they do not certify the entire
154-page proof. The first two reconstruct their geometry independently of
the supplied research model. Additional C++ probes and their exact scopes
are described in research/elementary-organising/FOLLOWUP.txt.

For the separate existing certificate/Lean proof, use the root README.
No full Lean or external-kernel replay was performed to prepare this branch.

See research/review-validation.txt for the checks run on this package.

FOURTEEN-CUBE CLASSIFICATION CERTIFICATES

The classification covers 349 normalized roots: the original 357-root cover
restricted to anchor multiplicity at most five, at total fourteen. The
independent physical multiplicity lemma excludes the other eight roots.
Neither the reduction nor its certificates assume the fifteen-cube theorem.

Selected proof route
  133 strictly projected exclusions:       228,264 instructions
  196 identity-projection exclusions:      446,427 instructions
   13 core covers and extension exclusions:  14,012 instructions
    7 terminal enumerations:                 24,396 instructions
  Total: 349 roots, 713,099 instructions, 7,535 checked blocks.
  The seven nonempty roots contain the 81 classification terminals.
  The thirteen core covers contain 710 smaller vectors, none extendible to
  an original fourteen-copy failure. These are not classification terminals.

Inputs
  five-colour-classification.json lists the 56 representatives, their Hall
  deficiencies, 81 terminal inventories, root indices and certificate hashes.
  five-colour-classification-certificates.tar.gz retains all 349 original Q5E1
  streams. The selected route uses 203; all original hashes remain checked.
  five-colour-classification-projections.json and .tar.gz retain 137 candidates.
  The selected route uses 133 zero-budget exclusions; core covers supersede
  the four budget-one candidates. Headers reconstruct from original bounds.
  five-colour-classification-cores.json and .tar.gz contain thirteen complete
  core covers, 710 extensions, core lists, root mappings and input hashes.
  FiveColour/EnumerationChunks contains the retained generated Lean sources.

Proof interfaces
  RootProjection proves deletion at arbitrary total N, to total N-b+s.
  root_projection_below retains y <= x coordinatewise; root_projection_at
  forgets that conclusion for exclusion-only clients. root_projection is
  the N=15 specialization used by the threshold proof.

  ProjectionBridge checks encoded headers and identifies their complete
  integer admissibility predicates. Headers.lean and HeaderChecks*.lean
  certify the 329 exclusion-only cases. ClassificationProjection transfers
  these Fast.check exclusions to the original fourteen-copy roots.

  CoreExtension handles thirteen more empty roots. Enumeration.Covers proves
  that the smaller vector belongs to a complete core list. Fast.check excludes
  each box with the core as lower bound and ALL original upper bounds, at
  total fourteen with the original budget. Additions may occur anywhere,
  including outside the budget set. Headers and calibration are shared, and
  extensions are batched within each root, not separate per-core modules.

  PackedEnumeration adds exact listed singleton leaves to the checked interval
  rules. Every reused checkpoint requires exact boundary agreement. Group-level
  coverage hides the choice of exclusion, core extension or terminal enumeration.
  EnumerationCoverage joins all 349 groups' cases. ClassificationReduction
  uses physical Hall coverage and the independent multiplicity bound;
  ClassificationCore checks terminal counts and failures; ClassificationOrbits
  checks global symmetries and inequivalence; Classification56 proves the
  unique-class characterization of physical failures.

Formats
  Q5E1: magic, 156-byte root header, then preorder instructions. Opcode 0
  closes a family (255 means inconsistent total bounds); opcode 1 splits
  its coordinate at a third-byte threshold; opcode 2 refines a family;
  opcode 3 accepts an exact listed singleton.
  Q5C1 covers use native family 0 for a combined total refinement. Translation
  expands it into existing empty-cut and full-cut steps; the production Q5E1
  reader checks all translated checkpoint bounds. The covers contain 8,288
  translated instructions, including 309 expanded mass-refinement steps.
  Q5L1 extensions have the ORIGINAL header, then a 75-byte core lower bound.
  Their 5,724 instructions use propagation only. Altered bounds, budgets,
  totals, core identities and unsupported opcodes are rejected. Lean checks
  the resulting existing Fast.check expressions independently.

Reproduction
  make check-classification-data WORKERS=2
    Reconstruct geometry, replay the core/extension certificates, run 26
    rejection controls, replay selected original/projected inputs, audit
    physical failures and orbits, and compare regenerated sources read-only.
  make classification-replay WORKERS=2
    Build all roots, the physical endpoint and its axiom audit. Lake validates
    caches. The driver records initial source hashes, dependencies and settings
    and checks their final identity. Logs are in build/classification-replay/.
    Partial runs are explicitly labelled. External comparison is separate.
  make classification-data WORKERS=2
    Regenerate retained sources after preparing all output in a temporary directory.

The example/orbit audit alone does not establish exhaustive coverage. A full
classification replay and endpoint audit are required. The successful sixteen-claim comparison includes the physical endpoint;
verification/optimized-comparator.json identifies the checked proof sources.
verification/classification.json retains the completed 349-root replay and
its eight-declaration physical-endpoint and shared-bridge axiom audit. The original 5,150,973-instruction route has
historical evidence in verification/classification-baseline.json; its hashes
identify those sources and do not certify subsequent replacements.

The Lean endpoint uses 56 concrete representatives. The symbolic 51+4+1 forms
and 11,430 labelled inventories follow from the separate normal-form/orbit
audit and mathematical arguments, not additional assertions of that endpoint.

Selection and provenance
  The 137 projected candidates were selected for native acceptance, correct
  reconstructed headers and strict instruction improvement over the original
  streams. The generator checks that comparison. No globally minimal or
  exhaustive discovery search is claimed.
  The thirteen core replacements are the supplied accepted zero-budget covers
  with checked exclusions of every original-bound extension. Their complete
  core lists are certified independently of the classification census.
  All checking inputs are retained. Bundle digests identify supplied research
  archives; those archives are not additional reproduction dependencies.
